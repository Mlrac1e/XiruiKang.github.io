About

I build models that connect perception, language, and action.

I am Xirui "Miracle" Kang. As an Embodied AI researcher, my work aims to give artificial intelligence the ability to understand and interact with the physical world. But beyond the algorithms, my true drive is deeply personal.

To me, life is an endless quest for understanding. I believe that through the quiet persistence of lifelong learning, we can gradually transcend the endless complexities of "how" (Shu) to grasp the profound clarity of "why" (Dao). Yet, the summit of this intellectual journey is never solitary wisdom. Ultimately, knowledge is a vessel meant to be emptied—shared freely to illuminate the path for others, translating our collective progress into genuine, enduring happiness.
